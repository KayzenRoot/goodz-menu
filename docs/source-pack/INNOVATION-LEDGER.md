## 1. Purpose

Preserve Goodz proprietary product concepts as governed engineering assets.

A trademark-style name does not prove novelty, patentability or technical success. Production claims require implementation and evidence.

## 2. States

- `PROPOSED` — idea recorded.
- `PLANNED` — canonical requirements/architecture admit it.
- `EXPERIMENTAL_GATED` — admitted only behind explicit proof gates.
- `PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE` — required proof gates passed for a production slice.
- `PROVEN` — shipped and outcome evidence validates the intended benefit.

## 3. Proof gates

Common gates:
- `U` Utility;
- `A` Assurance/correctness;
- `V` Validity/stability/invalidation;
- `R` Engineering ROI;
- `S` Security/privacy;
- `Q` Quality/performance;
- `E` Evaluation/outcome.

## 5. Promotion rules

A technology may move state only through governed evidence.

### PLANNED → EXPERIMENTAL_GATED
Used when the mechanism's benefit/correctness cannot be accepted purely from deterministic implementation.

### EXPERIMENTAL_GATED → PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE
Requires all named proof gates to pass for the admitted use case.

### PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE → PROVEN
Requires shipped outcome evidence over a defined observation window or benchmark population.

## 6. Anti-hype rule

Do not advertise:
- “proprietary AI” merely because a prompt exists;
- “self-learning” without a measured learning mechanism/outcome;
- “causal” when only correlation exists;
- “optimization” without objective/constraints/proof;
- “real-time” without measured latency;
- “autonomous” when actions are actually manual;
- guaranteed financial/investment returns.
