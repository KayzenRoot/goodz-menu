## Product mission
Build an AI-native operating platform for food businesses that does more than record transactions: it must help the owner understand the business, predict risk, simulate alternatives, recommend actions, execute only authorized actions, measure outcomes and learn.

## Initial operating context
The first real consumer is the owner's own food business. The architecture must nevertheless be SaaS-ready and multi-tenant from the beginning.

## Development target
- local development and continuous owner validation through Docker;
- later cloud/SaaS deployment through governed deployment planning;
- strong mobile/responsive support;
- full light/dark themes;
- high-quality design system with controlled glassmorphism, motion and polished feedback states.
